CRM & Sales Pipeline System

Centralized CRM automating lead capture, follow-ups, and revenue forecasting

Project Overview

Industry
SaaS Consulting
Team Size
6 Active Users
Timeline
4 Week Implementation

The Challenge

A growing SaaS consulting firm was managing leads across scattered spreadsheets, email threads, and personal notes. Sales reps had no visibility into pipeline health, follow-ups were inconsistent, and leadership couldn't forecast revenue accurately. They needed a single source of truth for all client data.

The Solution

We built a unified CRM system in monday.com that centralizes all client data and automates the sales process. The system features lead capture forms that feed directly into the CRM with auto-assignment, pipeline automations that trigger follow-ups and notifications based on status changes, sales dashboards showing real-time pipeline value, conversion rates, and rep metrics, and email integration that logs all client communication automatically.

Built with monday.com, n8n, and Gmail
